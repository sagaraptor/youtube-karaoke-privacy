Privacy Policy

YouTube Karaoke Left Right Switch

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Audio Processing

This extension processes audio from the current browser tab when you choose an audio mode. Audio processing takes place locally in your browser.

2. Data Collection

This extension does not collect or transmit personal information, browsing history, or audio recordings to external servers.

3. Data Storage

This extension does not store personal information or audio recordings.

4. Permissions

The extension uses browser permissions to access and process audio from the current tab for its audio-switching function.

5. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the extension developer through the Chrome Web Store.
